# The Lemme operating system — translated for Be Different Packaging

Distilled from the full 13-file playbook (Drive: Lemme playbook folder). This is
the working reference; the skills in `.claude/skills/` encode the parts the tool
executes. Lemme facts kept verbatim where they matter; every section ends with
the BDP translation. **Rule zero: copy the machine, not the overreach — Lemme is
fighting class actions over GLP-1 claims. Our honesty gates exist for a reason.**

## The five structural ideas

1. **The product name is the ad.** Every Lemme SKU is named after the desire
   ("Sleep", "Debloat", "Chill") — zero decode time, every mention is a benefit
   statement, and it mints owned search terms ("lemme debloat review").
   → BDP applies this to **CTAs and page names**, not SKUs — we sell formats
   (droppers, jars, bottles, pumps), so the format is the format. But every
   button, subject line CTA, and 404 uses the **"BE ___" verb-tic**: `BE
   STOCKED`, `BE BRANDED`, `BE ON REPEAT`, `BE LAUNCHED`, `BE FIRST`. Same
   effect as Lemme's `LEMME TRY / LEMME SUBSCRIBE`: turns every mention into
   an owned identity beat. The test: a stranger sees only the CTA for one
   second — do they know what happens next?
2. **Packaging is a media surface.** Legible at 150px thumbnail, recognizable
   in a 0.5s paused video frame, readable at 6 feet.
   → BDP sells packaging — this is literally our product pitch AND our content
   rule: every product photo must pass the "hand shot" test (held at arm's
   length, mixed lighting, benefit readable).
3. **Volume beats perfection in paid.** ~62 new Meta creatives/week from 7
   repeatable templates, 2–5 variants each; kill below account average at
   ~$100–150 spend. Lean-team floor: 8–15/week.
   → BDP now: same discipline on organic — templates in Studio/Quick Design,
   variants not hero posts. When ads switch on, the 7 formats are the library.
4. **Commission swarm beats sponsorship.** 13,000 commission-only creators
   averaging 4–5 videos each ($13M/month at peak). Recruit people who *have
   the problem*, 1K–100K followers; loose brief (3 talking points + forbidden
   claims); zero-strings gifting on top (no posting requirement — one
   unsponsored review hit 48M views).
   → BDP: the "creators" are **customer brands**. Feature-their-brand program:
   gift/discount packaging to indie skincare brands who post their packed
   products; referral commission for repeat referrers; repost everything (with
   permission). Their product shots ARE our proof content.
5. **Credibility is sequenced, not claimed.** Community → expert → marketplace
   badges → retail. Rent authority (named experts on PDPs/ads) as a media
   line item.
   → BDP: Etsy 4.9★ history → real customer brands on the site → (later) a
   cosmetics-formulator or packaging-engineer voice as the "expert" layer.

## Voice system (applies to every asset this tool produces)

- Human, never corporate — "a group-chat message from your funniest
  [packaging-obsessed] friend." Levity + facts coexist.
- Benefit in the first five words. One specific, visualizable moment per asset
  (their best hook: "Friday night's jeans don't fit today?" — ours: "Launch in
  three weeks and no bottles yet?").
- A verbal tic used everywhere (Lemme → "Lemme Sleep", CTA "Lemme Socialize").
  BDP candidate: **"Be Different"** as the sentence-starter — "Be Different.
  Start at 10." / CTA labels "Be stocked", "Be branded". Or lean on "bdp".
  Decide once, use it in nav, CTAs, email sign-offs, 404s.
- Voice checklist: real person would text it? · one visualizable moment? ·
  benefit in first 5 words? · every claim true and sourced? · sounds like us?

## The 7 Meta ad formats (also our social/static template library)

| # | Format | Share | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Feature/benefit pointout | 29% | Product centered, max 4 callouts, 2–3 words each, outcome language |
| 2 | Big headline | 25% | 4–8 words, all caps, identity or curiosity: "STRENGTH LOOKS GOOD ON YOU" → ours: "FROM 10 UNITS. NO EXCUSES." |
| 3 | Listicle / timeline | 10% | A: "what the numbers say"; B: Day 30/60/90 timeline → ours: order-journey timeline (today → dispatch → 4–6wk branded) |
| 4 | Fake UI / notification mockup | — | "Reminder: your [stock] is running low → [Already did] [Doing it now]" — replenishment gold for packaging reorders |
| 5 | Expert review static | — | Real credentialed person, first-person quote, credentials legible |
| 6 | Problem-question hook | — | `[hyper-specific moment this week]?` — "Bloated?" fails; "Friday night's jeans don't fit today?" wins |
| 7 | Lifestyle association | — | Product in the aspirational moment (their wedding-day Debloat) → ours: the packed shelf, the launch-day unboxing |

Visual system: one signature color per SKU/collection, gradient/pastel fields,
product hero-sized, big bold sans + a warm script accent, floating elements,
real-ingredient props → ours: real product-in-use props (droppers with serum,
amenity bottles on hotel linen). Template kit goal: new variant in <30 min
(Quick Design/Studio presets).

## The 5-beat video skeleton (30s; every video this brand makes)

1. **0–3s Question hook** — the felt problem as a question ("Launching a
   skincare line and packaging quotes are taking weeks?")
2. **3–8s Solution reveal** — product in hand, name it
3. **8–18s Mechanism/proof** — 2 named specifics, one function each (live
   tiered pricing → watch unit price drop; from 10 units → test before
   committing). Never vague.
4. **18–26s Friction removal** — how easy it fits ("pick, pay, packed — no
   quote round-trips")
5. **26–32s Identity close + CTA** — disown the category's pain: "…not another
   'request a quote' supplier."

Skins: talking-head (6–10 cuts), static-to-video (cheapest), customer
testimonial (licensed), founder story (the *reason* is the celebrity — Ivi's
"I sold on Etsy at 4.9 stars and hated how brands get treated at small
volumes, so…"). Hook bank: write 20, test 5/week; hooks are interchangeable
heads on a reusable beats-2–5 body — film once, splice. Cut a 12–15s version
(beats 1+2+5) for retargeting.

## Email & SMS (the part our tool already executes)

- **Capture:** two-step fullscreen popup + persistent teaser. Step 1 email
  only (+incentive), step 2 phone (skippable, separate consent). SMS field on
  step 1 cuts email capture up to ~50%. Healthy capture: 3–7% of uniques.
  → shop-side build.
- **Cadence: ~10 campaign emails/month** — 4 education/value · 3 product ·
  3 promo/replenishment. Alternate **designed** (Email Designer) ↔
  **plain-text founder notes 2–3×/month** ("can I ask you something?") — the
  pattern break drives outsized replies.
- **Flows** (shop + this tool): Welcome E1 now/E2 +2d/E3 +4d/E4 +6d ·
  Abandoned checkout SMS 1h → email 4h/24h/48h · Post-purchase confirmation →
  what-to-expect timeline → review ask +10d → affiliate/feature invite ·
  Replenishment at product-duration (Format-4 notification email) ·
  Back-in-stock (fast, SMS-worthy) · Winback 60–90d (plain-text first).
- **Subscription analog:** the standing repeat order — **10% off every order
  on a 3-month minimum commitment**. Sell the perks alongside the discount:
  guaranteed stock during sellouts, one invoice, cancel or change formats
  after the 3-month floor. Target: email+SMS = 25–35% of revenue at maturity;
  standing-order attach ≥ 25% by month 6. **No physical bundles as separate
  SKUs** — the subscription is the retention product.

## Organic cadence (lean team)

TikTok 1–2/day (original + reposted customer content) · Reels 4–6/week
(repurposed) · IG feed 3/week · Blog 2–4/month (each links to a product page).
Feed ratio: **~60% faces/stories · 25% education · 15% product/launch**.
Trend-jacking: 20 min/day logging trends, ship 2–3 adaptations/week while the
trend is <10 days old. Reply to comments with video replies; DM every organic
poster → feature → invite to the program. Quarterly: 1 real launch or format
twist + 1 narrative stunt.

## Site/PDP anatomy (shop-side reference, 10 parts in order)

Hero (benefit name, rating, one-line promise, subscribe-toggle defaulted) →
subscription widget with perks → story block → mechanism (named specifics,
one job each) → credibility rail (faces + credentials) → UGC wall → Day
30/60/90-style expectations timeline (ours: the order journey) → FAQ from real
objections → standing-order module (10% off + perks, 3-month minimum) →
disclaimer footer. Free-shipping threshold ≈ 1.3–1.5× AOV with cart progress
bar. Every page answers in order: what will this do for me (1s) → why believe
you (30s) → why is it easy.

## Google (when social flywheel spins)

Demand capture, not creation. Brand defense first (own "[bdp] review"
SERPs proactively), Shopping/PMax with benefit-first product titles, non-brand
problem keywords capped until brand search climbs. Budget early: ~70% brand +
Shopping · 20% non-brand tests · 10% retargeting. Diagnostic: flat brand-search
impressions = upstream social problem, not a Google problem.

## Compliance — what got Lemme sued, and our rules

They named a product after a drug class (GLP-1 Daily), claimed "clinically
studied" for ingredient studies that didn't test the formula (and showed no
weight change), and their own Instagram posts became court exhibits. Fine
print didn't save them: **net impression governs, not disclaimers.**

BDP's risk surface is smaller (packaging, not health claims) but the same 8
rules apply, translated: claims about lead times, stock, MOQs, and quality
must be literally true; "X-day shipping" only if we hit it; reviews only real
and permissioned; numbers with context; creators/customers we amplify are our
responsibility (brief them on what not to claim); one shared claims library so
site, ads, emails, and posts never drift. Our tool already enforces the sharp
end: [placeholder] gates, real-deadline-only urgency, no invented demand.

## 90-day sequence (adapted)

- **Phase 0 (wk 1–4):** naming/voice locked, shop PDP anatomy + popup + flows
  (shop session), template kit in Studio, claims library. KPI: popup ≥3%.
- **Phase 1 (wk 3–8):** seed the customer-brand swarm — gift/discount program
  to filtered indie brands (has-the-need filter), loose brief, repost engine,
  organic cadence live. KPI: brands activated/month, % posting ≤14 days.
- **Phase 2 (wk 6–12):** paid on — 8–15 creatives/week from the 7 formats,
  5-beat videos, kill/scale at ~$150; Google brand+Shopping; email at
  10/month. KPI: blended CAC vs margin, standing-order attach ≥25%.
- **Phase 3 (mo 4+):** quarterly hero push (one collection, one reframe angle,
  synchronized across every surface), zeitgeist angle refreshed quarterly,
  marketplace badges → retail conversations.

**North star: repeat purchase rate.** Lemme's real moat is repeat behavior,
not reach. For BDP that's the standing repeat order and the B2B account —
which is why the Outreach/Campaigns machine exists.
