# Anatomy of a Lemme campaign email (dissected from production HTML, email #96)

Source: real Klaviyo-built Lemme email ("Lemme Grow" hair gummy campaign),
600px-wide single column. Every block below appeared in this exact order.

## The 12-block skeleton

| # | Block | What it said | The job it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hidden preheader | "Lemme Grow supports thicker hair in 12 weeks*" (padded with invisible chars so nothing else leaks into inbox preview) | Wins the inbox: subject + preheader are one two-line ad |
| 2 | Offer bar (ABOVE the logo) | "FREE GIFTS FOR ORDERS OVER $85 & $115" | AOV ladder planted before content — two tiers, not one |
| 3 | Logo | lemme | Brand stamp, nothing else |
| 4 | Hero | "Thicker. Stronger. Fuller. BETTER HAIR BY FALL*" + SHOP CTA | Benefit trio (3 words) + seasonal deadline + one CTA |
| 5 | Credibility | Named ingredients, "clinically-tested/studied" language | Borrowed authority; every claim asterisked |
| 6 | Timeline | "What to expect: 30 DAYS …, 8 WEEKS …, 12 WEEKS …" + CTA | Kills the top objection (when will this work?) and sets expectations — fewer disappointments later |
| 7 | Proof | "The proof is in the pics. Real people. Real 12-week results." before/after + CTA | Evidence after education |
| 8 | Retention upsell | "Don't miss a month of growth… Subscribe & save up to 25%" + 4 perk bullets + CTA | Converts a one-time sale into recurring revenue INSIDE the acquisition email |
| 9 | Nav row | SHOP PRODUCTS / SUBSCRIPTIONS / LOYALTY REWARDS | Secondary paths for the not-ready |
| 10 | Social icons | IG/TikTok/Twitter/Facebook | Channel bridge |
| 11 | Legal | FDA disclaimer resolving every asterisk | Claims hygiene |
| 12 | Compliance footer | Unsubscribe link + full physical address, on brand-colored background | CAN-SPAM, styled — compliance treated as part of the design |

## Techniques worth copying

- **One product, one job, ~4 CTAs** — every CTA goes to the SAME destination.
  Multiple entry points, zero competing asks.
- **Benefit trio headline**: three punchy words, then a deadline with a season
  attached ("by fall" — always true, always urgent-ish, never fake).
- **The timeline block is the masterstroke**: concrete expectation per period.
  It sells AND pre-manages satisfaction.
- **Retention inside acquisition**: the subscribe-&-save block rides along in
  every product email. Recurring revenue is pitched at the moment of intent.
- **Full alt text on every image slice** — the email reads perfectly with
  images off; alt text mirrors the copy verbatim.
- **Every claim has an asterisk and every asterisk resolves** in block 11.

## Be Different Packaging translations (honest equivalents only)

| Lemme block | BDP version |
|---|---|
| Benefit trio | "From 10 units. Live pricing. No quote round-trips." |
| Seasonal deadline | "Stocked before [season/holiday] rush" — tied to real lead times |
| Clinical credibility | Real numbers: years supplying, order counts, Etsy 4.9★ history |
| Timeline block | Order journey: "Today: order online → [X] days: delivered (stock) → 4–6 weeks: custom-branded runs" |
| Before/after proof | Customer brands' products in our packaging; packing-bench shots |
| Free-gift AOV ladder | Free shipping over R___ / free sample kit over R___ (two tiers) |
| Subscribe & save | Standing repeat order: same order, on schedule, one invoice (the B2B backbone offer) |
| FDA disclaimer | Not needed — but resolve any footnoted claim (lead times, stock levels) |

## Hard rules that do NOT bend for inspiration

- Never fabricate scarcity, demand, reviews, or deadlines. Lemme's urgency is
  seasonal and real; ours must be too.
- Cold outreach NEVER uses this designed-campaign format — cold stays plain,
  personal, capped, in the app's review queue. This skeleton is for the
  opted-in list only (Resend Broadcasts, shop side).
- Marketing never sends from orders@ (transactional-only; enforced in code).
- Unsubscribe + physical address in every marketing email, no exceptions.
