# Email campaigns for Be Different Packaging

Read `references/lemme-anatomy.md` first — it's the dissected production email
this system is modeled on.

## Step 0: which lane? (never mix them)

| Lane | Who | Format | Where it's built |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cold B2B outreach** | Prospects who never opted in | Plain text, personal, signed Ivi, NO designed blocks | This app: Campaigns page → sequence steps → review queue |
| **Owned list** | Customers/subscribers who opted in | Lemme-style structured email (skeleton below), still lightweight | Resend Broadcasts (shop side) |

Cold email pretending to be a newsletter gets marked spam; a newsletter
written like a cold email wastes the trust already earned. Pick the lane
before writing a word.

## Step 1: assign the job

Every email has exactly ONE job: convert new subscribers · build belief with
skeptics · reactivate one-time buyers · drive AOV via bundles · launch beat
(tease / launch / momentum) · honest last-call. State the job in one sentence
before drafting. If you can't, don't write the email yet.

## Step 2: write to the skeleton (owned-list lane)

Order: preheader → offer bar → hero (benefit trio + real deadline + CTA) →
credibility (real numbers only) → timeline ("what to expect") → proof →
repeat-order/retention block → footer (unsubscribe + physical address).
Cut blocks that don't serve the job — never reorder them.

Copy formulas that carry the weight:
- **Benefit trio**: three short claims, full stops. "From 10 units. Live
  pricing. No quote round-trips."
- **Timeline**: 2–4 dated expectations ("Today you order → Thursday it ships
  → 4–6 weeks for branded runs"). This is the highest-leverage block for a
  packaging supplier — buyers fear lead times more than prices.
- **AOV ladder**: two thresholds, stated up top (free shipping over R X /
  free sample kit over R Y).
- **Retention rider**: every product email carries the standing-order pitch
  (same order, on schedule, one invoice).
- ~3–4 CTAs, ALL to one destination. One email, one link target.
- Subject + preheader are a single two-line ad; write them together, last.

## Step 2-alt: cold lane rules

Plain text only. First-name greeting, one genuine specific line (from real
research in prospect Notes — the app rejects unfilled [brackets]), one
question, price/MOQ hook, sign-off "Ivi". 3 emails max (+3d, +4d), stop on
reply. The app enforces: daily cap, send windows (ZA Mon–Fri 8:00–16:30 SAST,
US Tue–Thu 8:00–10:30 ET, UK Tue–Thu 8:30–10:30), suppression list, human
approval per message.

## Step 3: the honesty gate (blocks send)

- Every number, deadline, review, and stock claim must be true and current.
- No fake countdowns, no invented demand, no "clinically proven" cosplay —
  BDP's authority is real: order counts, Etsy 4.9★ history, lead times kept.
- Unsubscribe + physical address (16 Beach Road, Strand, Cape Town, 7140) in
  every marketing email. Marketing never sends from orders@.

## Cadence defaults

Owned list: 2–4/month — jobs spread across the month, not four sells in a
row. Launches get the 3-beat (tease → launch → momentum). Promos get the
2-beat (announce → honest last-call 24–48h before a REAL deadline).
Cold: whatever the app's engine paces out; never manually exceed it.
