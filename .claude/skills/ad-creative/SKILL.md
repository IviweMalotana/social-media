# Ad creative for Be Different Packaging

Source system: the Lemme teardown (`docs/LEMME-PLAYBOOK.md`). Portfolio of
repeatable formats, never hero campaigns. Volume with variants: every concept
ships as 2–5 variants (headline, crop, or background changes). Lean-team
target when paid is on: 8–15 new creatives/week across ≥4 formats; kill
anything below account average after ~R2,000–3,000 spend; scale winners.

## The 7 static formats (rotate products through, forever)

1. **Benefit pointout** — product centered, max 4 icon callouts, 2–3 words
   each, outcome language ("Test at 10" · "Live pricing" · "No quotes" ·
   "Ships fast").
2. **Big headline** — one packshot, 4–8 words, ALL CAPS, identity or
   curiosity. Templates: `[OUTCOME] LOOKS GOOD ON YOU` · `LESS [PAIN]. MORE
   [LIFE THING].` · `MADE FOR [EXCLUDED AUDIENCE] TOO.` Ours: "FROM 10 UNITS.
   NO EXCUSES." / "SMALL BRAND. SERIOUS PACKAGING."
3. **Listicle/timeline** — A: "what the numbers say" (real numbers only);
   B: the order-journey timeline (today → dispatch in X days → branded run in
   4–6 weeks). Timeline pre-sells the repeat order.
4. **Notification mockup** — phone-UI reminder: "Reminder: your 50ml stock is
   running low. Time to top up. → [Already did] [Doing it now]" — the
   replenishment format; prospecting AND retargeting.
5. **Expert/authority static** — real credentialed person, first-person
   quote, credentials legible. Until BDP retains an expert: the founder +
   4.9★ Etsy history is the authority layer. Never invent an expert.
6. **Problem-question hook** — `[hyper-specific moment this week]?` Generic
   fails ("Need packaging?"); specific wins ("Launching in three weeks and
   still no bottles?"). Keep a bank of 20 mined from real customer language.
7. **Lifestyle association** — product in the aspirational moment: the
   finished shelf, the launch-day unboxing, the hotel bathroom that looks
   five-star. Run alongside format 6: one sells the dream, one names the pain.

Visuals: one signature color per collection, product hero-sized, big bold
sans (Archivo feel) + minimal styling per the site brand, real product-in-use
props. Native CTA does the clicking — headlines sell, buttons don't.

## The 5-beat video skeleton (25–35s, every video)

1. **0–3s hook** — the felt problem as a question (first frame must work as a
   thumbnail: product visible or text readable)
2. **3–8s reveal** — product in hand, named
3. **8–18s mechanism** — exactly 2 named specifics, one function each (live
   tiered pricing; from-10-units testing; 4–6wk factory-direct branding).
   Specificity = credibility; never vague.
4. **18–26s friction removal** — how easy: pick, pay, packed. No quote
   round-trips.
5. **26–32s identity close + CTA** — disown the category pain: "…not another
   'request a quote' supplier."

Skins: talking-head (6–10 cuts) · static-to-video (cheapest) · customer
testimonial (licensed, real) · founder story (the *reason* is the celebrity).
Hooks are interchangeable heads on a reusable beats-2–5 body — film the body
once, splice new hooks. Also cut a 12–15s version (beats 1+2+5) for
retargeting. Captions always on, benefit words highlighted. 9:16 master,
crop 4:5 and 1:1.

## Hook formula and bank

`[specific moment] + [implicit question]`. Mine real customer emails/comments
for the moments. Seeds: "Launching in three weeks and no bottles yet?" ·
"Still buying 500 units to test one product?" · "Your supplier takes a week
to answer a quote?" · "Guests keep stealing the amenity bottles?" (that's a
compliment) · "POV: your first production run actually looks professional."

## Honesty rails (non-negotiable, enforced in this repo's culture)

Every number true; deadlines real; reviews real and permissioned; no invented
experts, demand, or scarcity; net impression governs — if a reasonable
scroller would expect something we can't deliver, rewrite it. Customer
content we amplify is our responsibility: brief them on what not to claim.
